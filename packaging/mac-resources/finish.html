Try ArrowKeys

Open Karabiner-Elements in Applications and complete its keyboard and driver permission prompts. Then open TextEdit, type a word, hold Caps Lock and tap J to move left.

To disable ArrowKeys, open Karabiner-Elements → Complex Modifications and remove the ArrowKeys rule. To enable it for another account, open /Library/Application Support/ArrowKeys/Enable ArrowKeys.command while signed in to that account.

To uninstall ArrowKeys, open Applications → Uninstall ArrowKeys.command.
